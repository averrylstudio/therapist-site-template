Welcome

Hello — I'm Jordan. I offer a steady, unhurried space for adults who are tired of holding everything together on their own.

I am a licensed clinical social worker. My work is warm, collaborative and trauma-informed. You are the expert on your life; I am here to help you listen to it more closely.

A moment, if you would like one

Take a breath with me

A short, guided breath in a 4–4–6 rhythm: in for four, hold for four, out for six. Use it before a session, or any time you need a quieter beat. You can pause whenever you like.

If you prefer less movement, turn on “calm” in the corner — the circle will stay still and the words will still guide you.
